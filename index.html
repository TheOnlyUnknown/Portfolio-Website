05 / 05 · Forecasting

India GDP NowcastingMixed-frequency Bayesian VAR

A nowcasting model that estimates Indian quarterly GDP growth in real time from five monthly indicators: industrial production (IIP), manufacturing PMI, Nifty 50, CPI and the RBI repo rate. It replicates the method of McCracken, Owyang & Sekhposyan (2020). Monthly data is stacked into quarterly blocks, giving a 43×17 observation matrix for a Bayesian VAR with a hand-implemented Minnesota prior and a closed-form Normal-Inverse-Wishart posterior. I tuned the shrinkage hyperparameter λ with a 200-iteration grid search. Once a full quarter of monthly data was available, it beat the random-walk baseline, and I scored its density forecasts with CRPS using bootstrapped residuals.

  • 8.87RMSE (random walk 9.72)
  • 7.74CRPS, density forecast
  • 43×17observation matrix
  • Bayesian VAR
  • Minnesota prior
  • Normal-Inverse-Wishart
